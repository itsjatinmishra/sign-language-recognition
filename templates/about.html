About Our Sign Language Recognition System

Bringing AI-driven communication solutions to the Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing Community.

What is this project about?

Our AI-powered system translates sign language gestures into text and speech in real-time. It helps individuals communicate more effectively using deep learning technologies.

How It Works

📷

Users **capture an image** or **use the live camera feed**.

🤖

The AI **analyzes the gesture** using trained deep learning models.

🔊

The **output is displayed** and can be converted to speech.

Why is this Important?

Millions of deaf individuals rely on sign language, but many struggle with communication barriers. Our system makes accessibility easier and **empowers inclusive conversations**.
